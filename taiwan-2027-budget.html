What is included and excluded

Included: the quoted airfare, travel tax, eSIM, insurance and Taipei room allocation; two Taichung nights; listed meals; both airport transfers; selected tours, admissions and transport; the selected reserve.

Excluded: shopping, souvenirs, hot-spring bathing, Monster Village, extra lanterns, extra forest-train rides, baggage charges, new travel clothes and any hotel quote shortfall. Lucky Land winnings and unconfirmed discounts are not deducted.

EasyCard: the NT$100 card purchase is an allowance. Loading NT$400 is moving money into a travel wallet, not an additional fare. Since each ride is budgeted on its travel day, that load is not counted again.

Seniors: local transport and attraction totals use full adult fares for everyone. Foreign senior eligibility differs by operator; only claim a discount after confirmation. Yehliu advertises a 65+ concession with proof; any saving is extra to this conservative budget.
